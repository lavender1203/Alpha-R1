alpha001 factor analysis report:
1. Factor formula: (rank(Ts_ArgMax(SignedPower(((returns < 0) ? stddev(returns, 20) : close), 2.), 5)) - 0.5). When returns are negative the factor takes the 20-day volatility, otherwise the close price; the series is squared (SignedPower, exponent 2), then the position of the maximum over the past 5 days (Ts_ArgMax) is cross-sectionally ranked and centered.
2. Mathematical logic and economic rationale: the factor builds a composite signal by conditioning on the sign of returns (volatility on down moves, price on up moves), amplifies extremes through squaring, and uses the recency of recent extremes as the stock-selection criterion. Economically it captures short-term mean reversion after price or volatility extremes: when the extreme occurred earlier (high factor value), risk has largely been released and a rebound is likely; when it is very recent (low factor value), short-term inertia or panic has not yet faded.
3. Market-regime effectiveness analysis: over the backtest window the factor's IC is positive most of the time with episodic negative phases, and NAV growth is limited. Effectiveness is clearly time-varying: it works in high-volatility, range-bound or structurally divergent markets, where it identifies short-term extremes cross-sectionally and earns reversal returns; it fails in one-way bull markets (persistent trends) and panic-driven cascading declines (unreleased fear), where reversal signals lag the trend, and in low-volatility low-turnover markets where cross-sectional dispersion is too weak.
4. Market environment features: strongest in range-bound / high-volatility regimes with high reversal probability after extremes; ineffective in trending bull or bear markets; during style rotation, performance improves when the rotation direction aligns with the factor's implied risk-appetite shift (e.g. from large-cap value to small-cap growth), and decays otherwise.
